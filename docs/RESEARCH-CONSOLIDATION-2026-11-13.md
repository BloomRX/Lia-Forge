# Research consolidation — 2026-11-13

This directory set consolidates the research deliverables from the independent Blender, Three.js, and T14 agent-ecosystem Arena branches onto a branch based on `main`.

## Included research

- Blender: `ECOSYSTEM-RESEARCH-BLENDER-ADDONS.md`, `BLENDER-ADDONS-MATRICES.md`, and `BLENDER-BRIDGE-SPECIFICATION.md`.
- Three.js: the complete `threejs-3d-environment/` research package (README and documents 01–17).
- Agent ecosystem: the four `T14-AGENT-*.md` research documents.

## Important status boundaries

These files are research, proposals, matrices, and specifications. Their presence here does not mean the proposed capabilities are implemented, tested, approved, or licensed for redistribution. Follow each document's own evidence and status labels.

The Blender local-file audit (the user's local add-ons and .blend files) is a separate pending activity and is not represented as completed by this consolidation.

## Deliberately excluded

- The T13/main agent's branch and its implementation commits.
- Shared application source, Electron adapters, scripts, tests, package manifests, assets, and other code copied into the secondary Arena branches.
- Full branch merges or cherry-picks.

The integration branch was created from `main` and only the research documents above were copied from the three secondary branches. Review before merging into `main`.
